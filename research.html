Top candidate: viability and test gate

The official USGS GeoDAWN record links a public magnetic/radiometric data release (DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ) and describes GeoTIFF products and CC0 rights. This verifies publication/access path, not that we acquired or checked the files or their exact channel schemas. Before testing rank 1, verify channel identity, coverage, license, grids and registration against the competition grid.

  1. Choose/record the method lane; get the authorized training stack, fault segments, sample grid, and registry rasters.
  2. On the proposed radiometric edge surface, calculate rank correlation and dot overlap with every registry raster. Stop and mark duplicate if the agreed thresholds are exceeded.
  3. Run the leakage canary feature by feature (AUC > 0.90 is leakage until explained).
  4. Use the shared evaluator for buffered whole-segment spatial holdout; derive catalogue features from visible faults only, mask them pixel-exactly, and score pooled DTI with alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, and 300 m triangular kernel.
  5. Report evaluator version, withheld positives and 95% CI. Compare against current holdout best; no real submission slot is selected in this research step.
