Its SHA-256 is `ab0a0a62eecf066a82713b09dd49f0f638a91fa3dd81f54cc34ae89afa3872be`.

**REGISTRY-MEASUREMENTS:** all 5,106,385 non-catalogue footprint cells are within its 3-pixel support halo; zero allowed cells are uncovered. For any nonempty candidate dot set D within that allowed domain, every element of D is already on the witness support, so

`forward_overlap(D, witness, radius=3 px) = |D| / |D| = 1`.

That exceeds the literal 0.70 stop threshold regardless of the geological method, rank correlation or holdout result. Empty/all-zero files do not solve the scientific task and must not be offered as unique high-scoring submissions. This conclusion needs only one hash- and grid-verified counterexample, not an exhaustive registry search. It does **not** establish that every candidate shares the witness's geology; it establishes that the requested support statistic cannot distinguish candidates from a full-support probability raster.

## Why H33 appeared to perform better

Fresh hash-pinned geometry checks establish an exact relation, not merely a visual resemblance:

- D2.8 has 44,090 positive cells; H33 has 37,654.
- H33 equals D2.8 **minus exactly 6,436 dots whose distance from the catalogue is at most 2 pixels**.
- Every removed dot is between 1 and 2 pixels from the catalogue. At the pinned 100 m grid resolution this is 100–200 m.

These are **RASTER-GEOMETRY-DIAGNOSTICS**, not scores. Reproduce with `python scripts/review_h33.py` after restoring its manifest inputs. [Strict-JSON evidence](../evidence/h33_geometry_review_2026-10-22.json).

The plausible metric explanation is reduced false-positive mass from unproductive near-catalogue dots, not a new geothermal discovery. Sparse emission can also avoid paying repeatedly for thick prediction bands. But removing a dot can reduce recall too; the direction of the metric change is not guaranteed without unknown organizer truth. Exact catalogue pixels are masked; nearby pixels are not automatically zero-credit. The DTI denominator changes with both coverage and prediction mass, so a leaderboard value cannot be linearly converted into mean per-dot credit.

The [pinned H33 owner page](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/f62038b54dcb7767d4d997f80b033cdc3d42bef9/docs/index.html) was retrieved afresh. Its historical note contains a projection and says **UNSCORED**; it is not a submission-page receipt. The user-reported H33 value and conflicting leaderboard figures are not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. We cannot explain a causal organizer-score improvement or promise to beat it from that evidence. We can formulate a falsifiable hide-and-recover hypothesis once the protocol permits experimentation.
