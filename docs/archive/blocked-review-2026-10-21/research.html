Top candidate: viability and holdout gate

Sentinel-1 SLC is the most methodologically distinct candidate found in the reviewed archive, but Copernicus' catalogue-level statement does not prove there are sufficient repeat SLC scenes for this exact Nevada footprint. Search the official catalogue by AOI and date, verify compatible repeat geometry and pair count, and inspect real products before proposing it as viable.

  1. Resolve the assigned method lane; obtain the authorized feature stack, fault components, sample submission and full registry inventory.
  2. Only after scene availability is verified, derive displacement/coherence surfaces without labels; compare the surface to every registry raster and stop if the rank-correlation or 3-px overlap threshold is exceeded.
  3. Run feature-alone leakage canaries; AUC above 0.90 is presumed leakage until explained.
  4. Use the shared buffered whole-segment holdout, visible-fault-only feature derivation and pixel-exact masking. Report evaluator version, withheld positives and 95% CI for pooled DTI (alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel).
  5. Compare against current holdout best; no weekly/final competition entry is selected in this research step.
