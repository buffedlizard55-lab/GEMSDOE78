Hypothesis, mechanism, mimic (preregistered before any result)

Hypothesis. Buried or blind normal faults that lack a mappable scarp still offset the subsurface across strike: on the downthrown side basement deepens, isostatic gravity and surface elevation fall, and conductive basin fill thickens. Paired flank-mean steps at +/-300, +/-600 and +/-1000 m across the regional strike, normalised by local variability, on magnetics, gravity, basement depth, topography and conductivity, together with explicit cross-layer sign-concordance features (shared throw direction), therefore recover withheld fault segments better than the pointwise steered-derivative learner (H78-A) under an identical emitter.

Mechanism. For each footprint pixel, with the strike-normal n and tangent t from the visible-catalogue strike field phi (orientation only, never location), sample Gaussian(sigma=1 px)-smoothed bands along the normal at offsets -10,-6,-3,0,3,6,10 px, each point averaged over tangent offsets -3,0,+3 px. Per band (det_elev, rtp, iso_grav_anom, depth_to_base_surf, cond_surf): signed step (v(+k)-v(-k))/(local sigma-10px std at centre + 0.05*global std) for k=3,6,10, its absolute value, and centre deviation (v(0)-(v(+6)+v(-6))/2)/denom signed + abs = 8 features x 5 bands = 40. Concordance from signed steps at k=6 and k=10 (magnetic step excluded from sign logic: anomaly sign depends on magnetisation, not throw): throw_z=(depth -elev -grav +cond)/4, abs, products -depth*elev, -depth*grav, elev*grav, depth*cond, and weakest-concordant min_i(o_i * sign(throw_z)) with o=(depth,-elev,-grav,cond) = 7 x 2 scales = 14. Total 54 STEP features. depth_to_base_surf enters via the stack's pinned log1p transform (data.py LOG_BANDS), disclosed.

Named non-fault mimic. Intrusive or lithologic contacts (magnetic + gravity step with no fault); basin-margin facies changes and buried paleochannels (conductivity / basement-depth step). The concordance block exists so no single band's step suffices: a contact usually lacks the elevation+gravity+depth throw-consistent combination, a paleochannel lacks the magnetic/gravity basement step.
