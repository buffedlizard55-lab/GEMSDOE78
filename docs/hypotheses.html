## Why h33-h33-2-b2 is the family best at 0.2778 [owner-report]

* Metric DTI = TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw), triangular kernel max(1−d/300m,0), one credit per truth pixel [official] https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/ . For binary dots, optimum is vertices 0/1 [measured] vendor/gems57/metric.py.
* Chain: H19-5 solid 121,131 px [measured] evidence/forensics_h33_vs_d28.json → D2.8 44,090 dots at ≥√8 px (2.83 px / 3.0 px median) [measured] → H33 37,654 dots (D2.8 minus 6,436 dots ≤2 px from catalogue) [measured] evidence/forensics_h33_vs_d28.json. Scores 0.1922→0.2600→0.2778 [owner-report] registry/family_scores.csv. Detector unchanged; budget geometry explains gain. Break-even credit per dot = 0.2·DTI ≈0.055 at 0.2778 [measured] vendor/gems57/metric.py.
* To beat 0.2778 the *field* must rank candidate pixels by more credit per dot on unmapped faults. Holdout best in this repo is A2 B3 0.1826 [measured-HOLDOUT-DTI evaluator gems57-pooled-hide-v2 seed20 11,321 withheld] evidence/h78_holdout_seed20.json.
